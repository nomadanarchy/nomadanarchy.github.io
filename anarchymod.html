Download UnblockBannedServers

UnblockBannedServers is a free, open-source Minecraft mod. Nomad Anarchy made it as a fork of AnarchyMod, the mod from 6b6t. It works with Fabric and Legacy Fabric and lets you join servers on Mojang's blocked server list. It runs entirely on your side.

How to install

  1. Install the appropriate mod loader for your Minecraft version:
    • Minecraft 1.14 and newer: Fabric Loader
    • Minecraft 1.13 and older: Legacy Fabric
  2. Download the UnblockBannedServers JAR matching your Minecraft version.
  3. Place the downloaded JAR into your Minecraft mods folder.
  4. Launch Minecraft using the corresponding Fabric or Legacy Fabric profile.
  5. Join play.nomadanarchy.online.
