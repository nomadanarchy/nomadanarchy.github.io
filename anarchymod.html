Download UnblockedBannedServers

UnblockedBannedServers is a free, open-source Minecraft mod forked by Nomad Anarchy from 6b6t. It supports Fabric and Legacy Fabric, allowing players to connect to servers on Mojang's blocked server list. It runs entirely on your side.

How to install

  1. Install the appropriate mod loader for your Minecraft version:
    • Minecraft 1.14 and newer: Fabric Loader
    • Minecraft 1.13 and older: Legacy Fabric
  2. Download the UnblockedBannedServers JAR matching your Minecraft version.
  3. Place the downloaded JAR into your Minecraft mods folder.
  4. Launch Minecraft using the corresponding Fabric or Legacy Fabric profile.
  5. Join play.nomadanarchy.online.
