The server

Server Information

Nomad is built around a simple idea: leave the world alone and let the players shape it.

The World

The map is persistent. Bases, ruins, highways, traps and monuments stay behind as players come and go.

Native server version: 1.12.2

No Rules

Griefing, stealing and exploits are part of the game. No land claims. No staff coming to save you.
